# Linker Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

Linker is a browser extension for creating and opening personal shortcuts to websites.

## Data Linker handles

Linker stores shortcut names and destination URLs that you create or import using Chrome's sync storage. Chrome Sync may synchronize this information between Chrome browsers signed in to your Google account, according to your Chrome Sync settings and Google's privacy terms.

When you open Linker's side panel, Linker reads the active tab's URL to offer it as a shortcut destination. It does not monitor your browsing in the background. Linker uses the shortcut information you provide to create browser redirect rules and open the destinations you chose. It does not read page contents.

Linker may request website favicons through Chrome's built-in favicon support to display icons for saved destinations.

## Sharing and retention

Linker does not send shortcut data to Taichikuji or a developer-operated server, and does not sell it. If Chrome Sync is enabled, Google may receive and synchronize shortcut data as part of the Chrome Sync service, according to your Chrome Sync settings and Google's privacy terms. You can delete shortcuts in Linker; synced data is also subject to your Google account's Chrome Sync retention and deletion settings.
